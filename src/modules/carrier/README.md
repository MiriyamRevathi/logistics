# Carrier Management & Compliance

## Vetting Protocol
1. **USDOT & MC Active Authority**: Minimum 90 days active operating authority.
2. **Insurance**: Minimum $1,000,000 Auto Liability & $100,000 Cargo.
3. **Safety Score**: No FMCSA BASIC category above 65th percentile.
4. **W-9 & Carrier Agreement**: Signed electronic agreement and verified Taxpayer ID.\n
